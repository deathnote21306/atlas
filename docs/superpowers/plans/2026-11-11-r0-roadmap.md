# ATLAS R0 Decomposition

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-16-atlas-prd-v2.md`

R0 is not one plan. It is ten subsystems, each of which can ship and be tested on its
own. This document fixes the order and says what each plan owns, so the plans can be
written one at a time rather than all up front.

## Why this order

Three rules drove the sequence.

1. **Honesty before depth.** The product currently renders ten countries at visually
   equal depth when their actual depth varies by an order of magnitude. Until coverage
   is labeled and provenance is on screen, every other feature inherits that credibility
   problem. Plans 1 and 3 are cheap and make everything after them defensible.
2. **Unblock the R0 gate early.** The gate is "at least one genuinely deep Full
   Intelligence market (initially Gabon)". Gabon does not exist in the dataset, so that
   gate cannot be met at any point until Plan 2 lands.
3. **Build the retention loop before the surfaces that consume it.** What Changed feeds
   the Monthly Brief, the watchlist and the alerts. Building those first would mean
   building them twice.

## What each plan owns

**1. Coverage status.** A `coverage_status` field on `country` with four values, a
`CoverageBadge` design-system primitive, and the badge rendered on the country list and
the country page header. Note that the existing `tier` column is A/B/C market tiering and
is a different axis; it stays as it is.

**2. Gabon.** Add GAB everywhere a tracked country is enumerated: seed, GDELT query terms,
`TRACKED_ISO3`, the spaCy `COUNTRY_MAP`, `ISO3_TO_CCY`, and the risk decomposition seed.
Ingest macro, FX and ratings. Gabon becomes the one country labeled Full Intelligence;
the existing ten drop to Standard or Benchmark according to what they actually carry.

**3. Sources & freshness.** The metadata in §15 already exists in `data_vintage`,
`macro_indicator_vintage` and `services/country/staleness.py`; almost none of it reaches
the screen. This plan surfaces source, period and retrieved-at per material metric, and
replaces silent blanks with explicit N/A per CI-004.

**4. What Changed.** A new `country_change` table and a detector that diffs macro
vintages, rating actions and FX moves, plus promotion of high-materiality graded news.
Renders high on the country page per WC-001, with the "No material change identified"
state from WC-005.

**5. The four missing pillars.** Politics & Institutions, Companies, Finance,
Infrastructure and Regulation have no models, no routers and no tabs. This is the largest
item in R0 and the one that converts Atlas from a macro tool into the business
intelligence product the PRD describes. Its plan will be split per pillar.

**6. Market Positioning & Peer Benchmark.** A configurable peer set per §20 rather than
the current hardcoded ten, and transparent Strong/Moderate/Weak plus
Improving/Stable/Deteriorating labels per §06B. This plan also resolves the two-risk-engine
defect, because §02 and §20 both rule out an opaque composite presented as truth.

**7. Monthly Brief.** Restructure the existing PDF to the nine prescribed sections. Four
of them cannot be written until plans 3, 4 and 6 land.

**8. Universal search & watchlists.** Permission filtering before results are returned per
SR-002, which is the part that has to be designed in rather than bolted on.

**9. Analytics.** The §17 event taxonomy, emitted from the start. Cheap now and
impossible to backfill, which is why §20 lists it as an immediate priority even though
nothing in R0 consumes it yet.

**10. Entitlements.** The four tiers in §07. Last because it gates features that have to
exist first.

## Explicitly deferred

Out of scope for R0 per §03 and §19, and not planned here: Government Enterprise,
Institution Private, Government Shared, project workspaces, approvals, external data
rooms, Opportunity Intelligence, Capital Activity, Market Interest Intelligence, Express
Interest and Private Enterprise. §19 marks collaboration and data rooms as "architecture
only" in R0, which the permission model in Plan 8 and Plan 10 is expected to accommodate
without building the workflows.

Also deferred, from §20's out-of-scope list and already present in the codebase: the
disabled **Deal Analysis** navigation entry should be removed rather than built, since a
DCF/IRR recommendation engine is explicitly out of scope. That removal is folded into
Plan 1.
